ENTERPRISE HUMAN RESOURCES: REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the guidelines and eligibility criteria for employees seeking remote or hybrid work arrangements.

2. Eligibility Criteria
- Full-time employees who have completed at least 90 days of continuous service.
- Roles classified as "Remote-Compatible" by departmental management.
- Demonstrated performance meeting or exceeding expectations during the previous quarter.

3. Working Hours & Core Availability
- Remote employees must maintain core working hours from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in their respective local time zone.
- Employees must be reachable via company Slack, Email, and video conference calls during core hours.
- A minimum internet speed of 50 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload is required for remote workstations.

4. Equipment Allowance
- The company provides a laptop, dual monitor setup, and a one-time ergonomic stipend of $500 for home office setup.
- All equipment remains property of the enterprise and must be returned upon employment termination.
