## 2. Session Demonstration Script (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Open the Application (0 – 1 min)
1. Launch the web UI in your browser at `http://localhost:8000`.
2. Point out the top status header showing **"❄️ Cortex Analyst: Active (Connected)"** and **"Status: HEALTHY"**.

> **Talking Point**: *"Most enterprise platforms force users to choose between querying a database or searching a document portal. Our Dual Intelligence Engine uses an automated query router to direct database analytics questions to Snowflake Cortex Analyst and document policy questions to Enterprise RAG in one unified chat interface."*

---

### Step 2: Demonstrate Snowflake Cortex Analyst (Structured SQL Analytics) (2 – 4 mins)
1. Click the sample prompt: **"Which city has the highest average customer revenue?"** (or type it in).
2. Hit **Execute Intelligence Query**.
3. **What to Highlight on Screen**:
   - **Auto-Routing Badge**: Explain how the engine identified intent keywords and routed the request to `❄️ Snowflake Cortex Analyst`.
   - **Executed Data Table**: Point out the live tabular dataset returned directly from Snowflake (e.g. `CITY: Mumbai`, `AVG_ANNUAL_REVENUE: 15,200,000`).
   - **Generated SQL Viewer**: Click *⚡ View Snowflake Generated SQL Statement & Confidence Metadata* to show the exact SQL generated by Cortex Analyst and executed on Snowflake.

> **Talking Point**: *"Notice how the user gets immediate tabular visual output—which is what business stakeholders need first—while developers can inspect the underlying Snowflake SQL statement and confidence metadata."*

---

### Step 3: Demonstrate Enterprise Document RAG (Unstructured Policy Retrieval) (4 – 6 mins)
1. Type the question: **"What is the password security policy?"**
2. Hit **Execute Intelligence Query**.
3. **What to Highlight on Screen**:
   - **Auto-Routing Badge**: Show that the router instantly switched to `📄 Enterprise Document RAG`.
   - **Synthesized Answer**: Point out the exact policy rules extracted (14 characters, 90-day expiration, MFA mandatory).
   - **Citations & Vector Chunks**: Highlight the source citation cards showing filename, chunk index, and cosine similarity score.
   - **Context Inspector**: Expand *📄 View Full Prompt & Context Inspector* to show the raw context block provided to the LLM.

---

### Step 4: Demonstrate Live Document Upload & Zero-Restart Indexing (6 – 8 mins)
1. In the left sidebar (*Document Ingestion*), click **Upload PDF / TXT / MD File** and select `hr_remote_work_guidelines.txt` (or drag and drop it).
2. Show the alert confirming instant chunking and vector storage.
3. Ask the question: **"What is the home office hardware stipend?"**
4. Show how the engine immediately retrieves the $500 home office hardware credit policy from the newly indexed file.
